EARNX MONETAG SETUP

1. Create/verify your site/domain in Monetag.
2. Create a MultiTag or another ad zone.
3. Copy the actual Monetag ad tag from Monetag.
4. Paste the tag in index.html immediately below <head> where the placeholder comment is.
5. For SmartLink, put your actual SmartLink URL in src/main.jsx:
   const MONETAG_SMARTLINK="YOUR_REAL_SMARTLINK";
6. Deploy again on Vercel.

Do NOT invent a zone ID or use somebody else's ad code.
Do NOT click your own ads or force users to click ads. Monetag controls ad delivery/frequency.

EARNX reward economics in this build:
50 points = ₹1
Minimum withdrawal = ₹25
Daily bonus = 25 points
Task rewards are configured by admin.

Your actual publisher income is NOT guaranteed. It depends on Monetag traffic, geography, ad format, fill, CPM/eCPM, advertiser demand and policy compliance.
