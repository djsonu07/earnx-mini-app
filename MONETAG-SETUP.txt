EARNX Monetag setup

Monetag SDK is installed in index.html:
<script src="//libtl.com/sdk.js" data-zone="11809504" data-sdk="show_11809504"></script>

Integrated ad formats:
1. In-App Interstitial (no reward): starts automatically 5 seconds after app load with:
   frequency: 2, capping: 0.1, interval: 30, timeout: 5, everyPage: false
2. Rewarded Popup: WATCH REWARDED POPUP button calls show_11809504('pop').
3. Rewarded Interstitial: WATCH REWARDED INTERSTITIAL button calls show_11809504().

Reward behavior:
- Rewarded Popup and Rewarded Interstitial award 20 points after the Monetag promise resolves successfully.
- 50 points = ₹1, so 20 points = ₹0.40.
- In-App Interstitial gives no reward.
- Do not self-click ads or encourage invalid/repeated clicks.
- Keep the reward amount within your Monetag program/publisher terms.
